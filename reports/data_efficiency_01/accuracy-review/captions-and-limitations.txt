01-learning-curves: Mean nodal Euclidean displacement error on all test geometries. Left: mean across three individual model seeds; error bars are sample SD across the three seed-level means, not confidence intervals. Right: arithmetic mean of three full-field predictions per architecture. All distances are in mm.

02-paired-differences: Graph minus pointwise mean nodal error; negative favors Graph. Intervals are the saved paired geometry-bootstrap 95% intervals. For individual models, seed errors are averaged within each geometry before resampling. These intervals are conditional and not simultaneous.

03-tail-errors (full report): Top: per-seed 95th percentile across geometry-level mean nodal errors, then averaged across seeds. Bottom: maximum nodal error across every geometry and (for individual models) every seed. Ensemble errors are computed from the averaged predictions, not averaged member errors.

04-computational-cost (full report): Single-model training and inference costs are averaged across three seeds. Ensemble training cost is the sum across members; inference is the measured sequential three-member forward on one GPU. Training is elapsed worker time including validation and checkpoint I/O, excluding setup/preprocessing; it is not utilization-weighted GPU time or total allocation walltime. Inference is warmed, synchronized, device-resident, batch-one full-field forward, excluding input transfer and preprocessing. Timings are descriptive saved means, not latency confidence intervals.

Accuracy table: Single entries mean +/- sample SD across seed means; no seed SD applies to the one equal-size ensemble. Cost/tail table: single-model Geom. P95 and worst-geometry mean are averaged over per-seed statistics; worst-node is the maximum over members. Mean geometry nodal P95, recorded separately in CSV, describes within-geometry node errors and is a different statistic from Geom. P95. Parameters are per model or summed for the ensemble.

Repeated benchmark evaluation: this test cohort was inspected in previous work. Results concern approximation of supplied discrete FE displacement labels on the supplied geometry distribution; no clinical, continuum-convergence or physics-informed claim. Geometry-bootstrap intervals are conditional on the fixed training subsets and three model seeds. They do not capture training-subset uncertainty or represent population-level anatomical validation. Intervals are pointwise 95% intervals without multiple-comparison adjustment.
