ADAPTIVE REFERENCE PILOT — separate numerical protocol

Purpose
Recover upstream of the endpoint anomalies without inheriting the late coarse
histories. This is a new pilot from rest through 0.35 s, not a continuation of or
replacement for the original 554-state reference. Native convergence and the
engineering tolerances below have not been established on the full model.

Unchanged physical inputs
Cached geometry, facet labels, fibers, passive constitutive model, density,
pressure ODE, weak boundary supports and alpha_m=.2 / alpha_f=.4 are retained.
Initial u and velocity are zero; initial acceleration is computed from the
continuous mass balance instead of resetting a late checkpoint's histories.
No material constant or force/positivity tolerance is relaxed.

Numerical recovery
- Use the native Jacobian with GMRES and host GAMG, CUDA matrix products, and an
  unshifted Newton direction. A failed or inadmissible solve rejects the entire
  tentative time interval; no normal-equation/local-ILU direction is used.
- Start with a macro interval of 0.000625 s. Compare one full step with two half
  steps. Compare the half-step pair at Q8 and Q10. If quadrature dominates the
  error, retry at Q10/Q12 from the same accepted histories before reducing time.
- Only the higher-quadrature pair is committed. Each tentative native solve
  requires custom force <1e-9 N, a zero-update native check <1e-8 N, and whole-cell
  positivity >1e-9 at both endpoint and stress stage. Native history updates are
  checked against the generalized-alpha/Newmark formulas.
- Maximum componentwise errors in u, v and a use relative tolerance 1e-3 and
  absolute tolerances 1e-7 m, 1e-5 m/s, 1e-2 m/s^2. The known hotspot patch has a
  separate maximum deformation-gradient error with atol=rtol=1e-3. These are
  declared pilot numerical controls, not physical acceptance thresholds or an
  established global error bound. The patch does not cover every cell; the force
  field comparisons and positivity proof retain global scope.
- Endpoint/stress-stage changes are limited to .25 in log(det(F)) and .5 in the
  isotropic material exponential argument at the monitored patch points. These
  controls limit per-step resolution error, not the absolute material response.
- Reduce macro dt by two when temporal error, stage change or nonlinear failure
  requires it; minimum macro dt is the original dt/64. Two very accurate pairs
  allow growth, capped at the original macro dt. Unresolved quadrature at Q12 or
  a failure at the minimum dt stops the pilot with its accepted prefix preserved.

Forcing
All allowed steps are dyadic. The native pressure ODE is evaluated on the exact
fifth-subdivision lattice of the smallest physical substep. New loading values
are not interpolated from the coarse reference. Original loading reproduction
near states 550-555 must pass before solving.

Recovery and qualification
Acceptance records are registered only after both checkpoints are durably saved.
A kill between JSON creation and registration leaves a tentative tail, which is
not counted as accepted. Accepted pairs remain available for explicit resume.
An eight-hour computational slice budget pauses the pilot; no successor is
submitted automatically. PBS resources remain 28 CPUs, 240 GB, four GPUs and a
168-hour allocation limit. The computational budget is checked at collective
solver and control-loop boundaries; it is not a watchdog against a stalled MPI
runtime. Such a stall still requires operator intervention.

A planned budget pause or an interrupted allocation may resume after the PBS
idle-job check. A completed numerical failure does not resume automatically. The physical model, code, source data and
forcing hashes must still match. Resuming requires an explicit command.

At the target time, fresh serial CPU reassembly verifies force, positivity and
histories for the last three accepted pairs (six consecutive physical substeps).
The full accepted chain retains hashes and the temporal/quadrature diagnostics.
This is a bounded pilot qualification, not proof of full-cycle mesh/time
convergence and not admission to the original reference or downstream dataset.
